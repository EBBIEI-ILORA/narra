How it works

Three steps to perfect notes

Designed for the real workflow of a busy mental health clinic. No extra software, no complex integrations.

01
Input your shorthand
Type, speak, or upload your post-session notes in whatever shorthand your clinic uses. NarraScribe understands clinical abbreviations, fragments, and your custom terminology.
02
AI expands and structures
Claude AI — operating under a signed HIPAA BAA — transforms your shorthand into complete, professional clinical language structured in your preferred format: SOAP, DAP, BIRP, or GIRP.
03
Review, approve & submit
The clinician reviews the generated note, makes any edits, digitally signs, and submits. Full audit trail maintained. Insurance-ready documentation in under 3 minutes.
