Our story

Built for the people who listen

NarraScribe was created to give mental health professionals what they deserve — time.

Why NarraScribe exists

Mental health therapists are among the most dedicated professionals in healthcare. They listen deeply, hold space for their clients, and carry an emotional weight that most of us will never fully understand. And yet, after every session, they sit down to write. For hours.

Clinical documentation is a necessary part of the work — it protects clients, supports insurance reimbursement, and creates a record that informs future care. But the time it takes is time stolen from rest, from preparation, and from the lives therapists are trying to live outside the office.

"My therapists used to dread note completion. Now they finish notes before they even leave the building."

NarraScribe was built to solve this. By combining the intelligence of AI with the real workflows of mental health clinics, we turn post-session shorthand into complete, insurance-ready clinical notes in seconds — not hours.

What we believe

Clinicians first
Every decision we make is filtered through one question: does this make a therapist's day better?
Privacy without compromise
HIPAA compliance and data security are not features — they are the foundation everything else is built on.
AI as a tool, not a replacement
The therapist always reviews, edits, and signs. NarraScribe assists — it never replaces clinical judgment.
Built for Maryland
We understand COMAR requirements, Maryland behavioral health standards, and the specific needs of Mid-Atlantic practices.

The mission

To give mental health professionals back the time they spend on documentation — so they can spend it on what actually matters: their clients, their practice, and themselves.
