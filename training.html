Why NarraScribe
What makes us different from every other documentation tool on the market.
Custom shorthand dictionary
NarraScribe learns your clinic's unique shorthand. No other platform lets you build a custom abbreviation library that auto-expands exactly the way your clinicians write.
CMS-1500 claim prep built in
Every note connects directly to a pre-filled CMS-1500 claim form. Provider NPI, CPT codes, diagnosis codes, and place of service — all auto-populated. No other documentation tool does this.
All 50 states compliance built in
State-specific recording consent laws, minor consent rules, 42 CFR Part 2 substance-use protections, and data retention requirements — all handled automatically per clinic location.
Built for behavioral health — not general medicine
DSM-5 diagnosis codes, behavioral health CPT codes, mental health POS codes, supervisor sign-off workflows, and 48-hour audit compliance tracking — purpose-built for mental health practices.
Clinic-level management — not just per user
Clinic owners manage their full team from one dashboard — therapist roster, compliance tracking, performance reports, and shared shorthand dictionaries. Competitors charge per seat with no clinic oversight.
HIPAA-compliant with signed BAA
Every clinic receives a signed Business Associate Agreement. AES-256 encryption, audit logging, role-based access control, and PHI isolation per clinic — built in from day one, not bolted on.
